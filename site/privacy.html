Privacy policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

WrongTrain is a group trip planner. This page explains, in plain words, what the app and this website collect, why, who else handles it, and how to remove it. We don't show ads, we don't sell your information, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in. We email you a 6-digit code; there are no passwords.
  • Your name and, if you choose, a profile picture, shown to the people on trips you share.
  • What you put into a trip: days, plans, places, travel, to-dos, notes, documents, expenses and photos. A trip you don't share stays on your phone. A shared trip is stored on our servers so the people on it can see it.
  • Your location, only when you ask. "Show my location" uses it on your phone only. "Where everyone is" shares it with the people on that trip, for the length of time you pick, and it's deleted when that time ends or you stop sharing.
  • Comments you send us about the app, with the app's build number and the kind of phone, so we can fix what you report.
  • How much of the AI features a trip has used (counts and sizes, not your words), to keep usage within limits.

Some things never leave your phone: your private notes, private documents (such as passport scans you mark private), your favourites, and your settings.

Who sees it

On a shared trip, the people on that trip see its contents, photos, comments and the names and pictures of the others on it. Trip owners decide who can change the plan. Nobody outside the trip can see it.

Services that handle it for us

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage (shared trips, photos and documents).
  • Google (Gmail) sends the sign-in code emails.
  • Anthropic (Claude) powers the AI features. When you use one, the text it needs is sent to Anthropic: a note you add and the trip it goes into, the answers to "Plan a trip for me", or a list of places near you for "Something nearby". Anthropic processes it to give the answer, under its commercial terms, which don't allow using it to train its models.
  • MapTiler draws the maps and looks up where places are; it receives the map area you're viewing and place names.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass) finds places for "Something nearby"; it receives the area around you, not who you are.
  • Frankfurter provides currency rates; it receives no personal information.
  • Apple (TestFlight) and GitHub (which hosts this website) see the technical information any app or website host sees.

Partner links

Some suggestions link to partners (for example tours, rail passes or travel insurance). If you follow one and buy something, the partner learns that you came from WrongTrain, and we may earn a commission at no cost to you. These links are always labelled, and we don't share your trip or your details with partners.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and shared trips until you delete them. Shared locations are deleted when the sharing time ends. When you delete your account, your sign-in, name, picture and membership of trips are deleted; things you added to a trip that others share may stay part of that trip for them.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time in the app: Account & settings → Delete my account.
  • Leave a trip, or as its owner delete it for everyone, in Trip settings.
  • Turn location sharing off whenever you like.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, at the address below.

Children

WrongTrain is made for adults and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change matters.
