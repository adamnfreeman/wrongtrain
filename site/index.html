Plan the trip together. Then actually go.

WrongTrain is one shared trip for your whole group: the days, the places, the trains, the money and the photos. Anyone can add anything, in plain words, and it lands where it belongs.

Made for groups of grown-up friends; no kids' menus included.

Everything the group needs, in one place

No more spreadsheet, group chat and five booking apps. WrongTrain keeps the plan, and everyone on the trip sees changes the moment they happen, even offline once loaded.

Just say it

"Dinner at Ichiran on day 3." "Our flight home is JL 4 at 7:45." Type it however it comes out; WrongTrain files it as a place, a plan, travel or a to-do, and fits it into the right day.

Plan it for me

Answer a few questions (where, how long, budget, what you're into) and get a plan: which cities, how many nights, where to stay, what to eat and see. Then change anything.

Right now

When a train or flight is coming up, the app shows exactly what you need in huge type: when to leave, which train, your seats, and your ticket one tap away.

Together, but your own

Everyone sees the whole plan. Say you're in or out of each thing, split off for an afternoon, and see where your friends are on the map when they choose to share.

Money, settled

Add what you paid in any currency, see who owes whom, and settle up with a tap. A currency converter that works offline, too.

Photos and phrases

Everyone's photos in one album, with hearts and comments. Useful phrases in ten languages, read aloud, with the ones you need starred.

A look inside

From a real (made-up) ten days through Kyoto, Nara, Osaka and Tokyo.

How it works

Started by a group of friends planning Japan, after one of us got on the wrong train.

  1. Start a tripHave WrongTrain plan it for you, or put in the cities and dates yourself.
  2. Invite your friendsSend a code. Everyone joins the same trip and sees changes live. You decide who can change the plan.
  3. Add as you goPlaces, bookings, tickets, ideas and photos, from anyone, filed where they belong.
  4. GoOn the trip, Today shows what's next and how long you have; Right now steps in when a train or flight is close.
